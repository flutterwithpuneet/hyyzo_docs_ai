# About Hyyzo

## Overview

Hyyzo is an AI-powered productivity platform designed to help teams collaborate, automate workflows, and manage knowledge efficiently.

## Mission

Our mission is to empower organizations with intelligent tools that reduce manual overhead, surface critical insights, and accelerate decision-making.

## Company

Founded in 2024, Hyyzo is built by a team of AI engineers, product designers, and enterprise software veterans committed to delivering simple, powerful, and secure solutions.

## Core Values

- **Intelligence First** — Every feature is enhanced by AI to deliver smarter outcomes.
- **Privacy by Design** — User data is never shared or used for model training without explicit consent.
- **Simplicity** — Complex technology should feel effortless to use.
- **Transparency** — Open documentation, clear pricing, and honest communication.
